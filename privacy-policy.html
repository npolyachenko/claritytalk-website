ClarityTalk Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Introduction

ClarityTalk ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains in detail how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to transparency and compliance with all applicable data protection laws including GDPR, CCPA, and other regional privacy regulations.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the App. By using ClarityTalk, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Data Controller

For the purposes of data protection law, the data controller is:

  • Organization: ClarityTalk
  • Contact: npoliachenko@claritytalk.org

Information We Collect

Audio Data

With your explicit consent, ClarityTalk records and analyzes audio conversations for the purpose of providing communication insights. Audio recordings are:

  • Only collected when you actively choose to record a conversation
  • Processed using AI to generate insights and recommendations
  • Stored securely on your device and in encrypted cloud storage
  • Never shared with third parties without your explicit consent
  • Can be deleted at any time from within the App

Analysis Data

When we analyze your conversations, we collect:

  • Transcribed text from audio recordings
  • Emotional tone and sentiment analysis
  • Communication pattern metrics (speaking time, interruptions, etc.)
  • Rapport and engagement indicators

Usage Data

We automatically collect certain information when you use the App:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version and usage statistics
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • Analytics data to improve the App experience

Account Information

If you create an account, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Name (optional)
  • Profile preferences

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide AI-powered conversation analysis and insights
  • Generate personalized communication recommendations
  • Improve and optimize the App's features and performance
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Send you technical notices and updates
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues

Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Audio recordings are encrypted both in transit and at rest
  • All data transmission uses industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security audits and updates

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide you with our services and as described in this Privacy Policy. You can delete your conversations and analysis data at any time from within the App.

If you delete your account, we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or regulatory purposes.

Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your data and account
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of data collection (though this may limit functionality)
  • Withdraw consent for audio recording at any time

Third-Party Services

ClarityTalk uses the following third-party services to provide and improve our App. Your data is shared with these services only as necessary to provide the core functionality of the App:

AI and Speech Analysis Services

  • OpenAI: We use OpenAI's APIs to perform conversation analysis, generate insights, and provide recommendations. Audio transcripts and text data are sent to OpenAI for processing. OpenAI processes this data according to their API data usage policies and does not use API data to train their models. For more information, see OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • Hume AI: We use Hume AI for advanced emotional intelligence analysis and speech emotion recognition. Audio recordings may be sent to Hume AI for processing emotional tone, vocal patterns, and sentiment analysis. Hume AI processes data in accordance with their privacy policy. For more information, see Hume AI's Privacy Policy.
  • Speech Recognition Services: We may use Apple's Speech Recognition framework or similar services to transcribe audio into text. This processing may occur on-device or via Apple's servers depending on your device settings.

Cloud Infrastructure and Storage

  • Cloud Storage: Audio recordings and analysis data may be stored using secure cloud services (such as AWS, Google Cloud, or iCloud). All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest.
  • Database Services: We use secure database services to store user accounts, preferences, and conversation history.

Analytics and Performance Monitoring

  • Analytics Services: We may use analytics services (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or similar) to understand how users interact with the App, identify bugs, and improve performance. These services collect anonymized usage data.
  • Crash Reporting: We use crash reporting tools to identify and fix technical issues. These tools may collect device information and error logs.

Payment Processing (if applicable)

  • In-App Purchases: All payments are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information. Payment processing is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Important: These third parties have access only to information necessary to perform their specific functions and are contractually obligated not to disclose or use it for other purposes. We conduct due diligence on all third-party service providers to ensure they maintain appropriate security and privacy standards.

Data Processing Agreements

We have data processing agreements in place with all third-party service providers that process personal data on our behalf. These agreements ensure compliance with applicable data protection laws and include provisions for:

  • Confidentiality and security of data
  • Processing only according to our instructions
  • Deletion of data when no longer needed
  • Notification of data breaches

Children's Privacy

ClarityTalk is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. These countries may have different data protection laws. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to know if personal information is sold or disclosed
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information
  • Right to deletion of personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

Note: ClarityTalk does not sell your personal information.

GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing
  • Rights related to automated decision-making

Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will:

  • Notify you via email within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach
  • Describe the nature of the breach and the data affected
  • Provide information about steps we are taking to address the breach
  • Advise you on steps you can take to protect yourself
  • Notify relevant data protection authorities as required by law

Data Portability

You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format. You can export your data from within the App at any time. Exported data will include:

  • Your account information
  • Conversation transcripts (if saved)
  • Analysis reports and insights
  • Usage history and preferences

Automated Decision-Making

ClarityTalk uses AI-powered automated analysis to generate insights and recommendations about your conversations. This automated processing:

  • Does not make legally binding decisions about you
  • Provides suggestions and insights for your consideration
  • Can be reviewed and questioned by contacting our support team
  • Is based on objective communication patterns and emotional analysis

You have the right to object to automated decision-making and request human review of any AI-generated insights.

Recording Consent and Legal Compliance

Important Legal Notice: You are solely responsible for ensuring that you have legal authority to record conversations processed through ClarityTalk. Recording laws vary by jurisdiction:

  • One-Party Consent: Some jurisdictions only require one party (you) to consent to recording
  • Two-Party Consent: Other jurisdictions require all parties to consent to recording
  • Your Responsibility: You must comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws regarding recording

ClarityTalk is not responsible for your compliance with recording laws. We strongly recommend obtaining explicit consent from all parties before recording any conversation. Unauthorized recording may result in civil or criminal penalties.

  • Financial information
  • Personal relationships and family matters
  • Employment information
  • Other confidential or sensitive topics

We treat all conversation data with the highest level of security and confidentiality. However, we recommend being mindful of discussing highly sensitive information and considering whether recording is appropriate for such conversations.

Anonymized data cannot be traced back to individual users and does not contain any personally identifiable information. We never share individual conversation recordings or transcripts for research purposes.
